01. About Me

I'm a software engineer based in Seville, with a degree in Software Engineering from the University of Seville and a Master's in Computational Logic and Artificial Intelligence currently in progress. My end-of-degree project tackled video game image recognition using a convolutional neural network.

On the professional side, I spent two years as a Test Means Engineer at Airtificial, building fullstack .NET applications and Oracle SQL databases for production stations in the aeronautics sector. In parallel, I led the E-Powertrain & Electronic Systems team at eUS Racing for seven years — combining Python ECU programming with automotive electronics and team leadership.
